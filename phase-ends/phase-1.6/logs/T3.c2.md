## Why the mirror leg
- A wrong equate is self-consistent in the listing alone: names.inc and the substitution read the same table, so the name just appears where the operand equals the wrong addr (same bytes) and the true site stays raw hex -> both self-checks still match. The firing leg for a planted wrong equate is names <-> symbols.tsv agreement (symbols.tsv was mirrored from the true names); the de-naming leg catches a symbols.tsv/listing divergence.
