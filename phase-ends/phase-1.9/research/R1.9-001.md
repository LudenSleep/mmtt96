Public web sources are thin: no manual text, no GameFAQs track list, no pad-level menu instructions found. Only mode descriptions and rule fragments below.

## Findings
- Construction Kit: exists; saves up to 10 tracks to cartridge internal memory (Sega Retro; LaunchBox says 9) or as a character-string code. Sega-16 says saved to J-Cart NVR chip. Can test while building. Menu path/buttons: NOT FOUND.
- Tracks: 60+ (Sega-16, Codemasters blurb) or 85 with 40 new (Sega Retro). Settings: gutters, bath, billiards/pool tables, labs, gym, camp site, BBQ. Pool table with F1 cars is a reviewer favourite (Sega-16 review of MM2:TT, likely carried over; unverified for '96). Race-order list: NOT FOUND.
- Head to Head (sega-16 MM2 review, same rules assumed): 4 dots/lights each; win a dot by getting a car off-screen edge; win by taking all 4 or most dots after 3 laps (MM2 text, unclear for '96). Tie-break: NOT FOUND.
- Challenge: 3 AI opponents, must finish first (top 2 per original MM series; here "finishing first" per Giant Bomb) to advance. Tie-break: NOT FOUND.
- TT Challenge: same tracks as Challenge, vs clock, 3 laps, time to beat (Giant Bomb). TT Single Race: one-off race on "the track of your choice"; 2P version fastest time wins (Giant Bomb). So yes, track choice is implied.
- 2P TT Tournament: first to 3 wins in a themed tournament of your choice. Tournaments listed: Jets'n'Jumps, Mighty Mix, Monster Course, Turbo Cars, Pro Tournament, Mega Comp, Rookie Races.
- Multiplayer: J-Cart has 2 extra ports (4 pads); up to 8 players by sharing pads. Menu selection of 3-4 players, whether P2 presses Start: NOT FOUND.
- Cheats: only Action Replay race-modifier codes (almarsguides), e.g. 0 Laps Remaining P1 FFD164:0000. No track select code found.
- tasvideos 6904M: modes Challenge, Head To Head, Single Race, League, Time Trial variants, Pro versions.
