## Gotchas
- Maps are column-major (consistent with T22.c1): map-field byte stride 8 = next row, nibble = column; slot y gets the
  row coordinate. Do not read the 128 px step pairing as an axis swap.
- generalizable: propagate refuses a name when the anchor's signature group holds an already-named routine
  (fn_001978 with race_main: both "bsr setup; clear 3 words; rts"); a kit-specific name needs a group split.
- 0x001DF8: the fourth kind-5 variant compare has no branch (nop); variant 3 falls through to the last record anyway.
- Eight-car game (code_004D34, Rules/Collisions groups, not doc'd here): each pad byte is split into a partner form
  (d-pad left/right bits kept, bit 4 set) and a main form (buttons A/C/B to bits 2/3/6, bit 4 set): hypothesis that each
  player drives two cars, one by d-pad and one by buttons; ram_playoff_timer is reused as a 4-cars-out sound timer and
  gates the 28-pair test (>= 25). Candidates for the Rules group.
