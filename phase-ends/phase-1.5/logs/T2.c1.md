## Decisions
- Manifest region record = keys ⊇ start/end/id/kind/sha1, no `parent`, kind not decoded/medium. TSV id maps to manifest `name` (manifest `id` is `r<nnnn>`); empty sha1 falls back to slice sha1.
- Swept = final sweep pos >= end (an insn crossing the end still counts swept; boundaries.py owns truncation).
- Family members may include exact dups (A,F dup + B family -> family {A,B,F}); unique tail excludes both.
- Fixture cases: big0 count dup family partition reach swept unique; files under `.run/census/fixture/`; loaded through the same loaders.
- Detail: `.run/census/{regions.tsv,big0.txt,summary.txt}`; no timestamps -> deterministic.
