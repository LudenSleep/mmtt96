tags: 68000,megadrive,vdp,ghidra,constants · date: 2026-10-16 · phase/task: 1.9/T2,T3 · origin: T2,T3 summary gotchas

# Classifying hardware immediates on 68000 / Mega Drive

- Ghidra prints 68000 immediates signed (`#-0x7300` is $8D00): mask by the operation size before classifying.
- An address register loaded once at boot (here a6 = VDP control) defeats per-region tracking: declare it as a checked
  invariant row rather than guessing per routine.
- VDP DMA source registers 15-17 may encode the RAM mirror (FExxxx): fold E00000-FFFFFF to FF0000 | low 16 bits.

Evidence: phase 1.9 T3 (33, 34), T2 (35).
