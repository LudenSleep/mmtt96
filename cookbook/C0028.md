# A claim checker for prose comments

Prose comments drift from the code. Check only claim forms with a fixed grammar, never free text:
- names, hex addresses and ranges must exist in the name/region tables;
- group membership, `one of N`, `N such ... at`, coverage counts are recomputed from the tables;
- anything outside the grammar is ignored (not passed, not failed).

Keep a bad fixture with one planted wrong row per claim form and a self-test asserting each is caught by its intended
check (not just "some failure"); wire both the check and the self-test as health rungs. Source: 1.7/T8
(`scripts/census/commentcheck.py`, 12/12 planted claims caught).
