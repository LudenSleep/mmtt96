tags: ghidra,comments,roundtrip,gotcha · date: 2026-10-16 · phase/task: 1.9/T12,T30,T31 · origin: T12,T30,T31 summary gotchas

# comments.tsv order: eol before plate at one address

`db.py export` writes rows sorted by address, and at one address the eol row before the plate row. Any script that
merges or sorts `comments.tsv` must keep that order, or `db.py roundtrip` differs.

Evidence: phase 1.9 T12 (53), T30 (23), T31 (24).
